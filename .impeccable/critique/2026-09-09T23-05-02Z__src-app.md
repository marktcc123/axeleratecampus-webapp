## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment.** A fluent product user would not call this a template, but would pause three or four times at things that look *unfinished* rather than strange. The hand-drawn system is genuine identity where each object is a different object: the tracker's overlapping status-tabbed folders, ticket stubs with punched notches, the taped sticky-note earn dock, highlighter blocks under cover titles, the flip-clock drop, hatched MarkerBars. It is decoration on a stock pattern on Me (five caps eyebrows over chevron rows plus two KPI cards — the closest the app gets to the "enterprise admin" anti-reference), on the shop grid (six identical cards, trust-badge trio, star reviews) and on the brand row (six letter discs). The strongest "pause" is content: covers are unrelated stock posters (a São Paulo summit flyer illustrating a Solra unboxing), guests are dashed squares, brands are initials — the board reads as a very good prototype. Layout sameness: three tab screens open identically (header → DynaPuff heading → folder tabs on a cream panel), and DynaPuff — meant to be "one per screen, the h1" — now sits on every h2 (BRANDS / EVENTS / MISSIONS; WEEKLY DROP over PERKS SHOP; MY WALLET / HISTORY), so no block leads.

Shared bans: side-stripe accents none; gradient text none (logo SVG only); glassmorphism none; hero-metric template borderline (Me's two KPI cards, the dock's CASH/CREDIT/XP row saved by being a sticky note); identical card grids partial (shop, brand row); **tiny uppercase eyebrows above every section: hit** (10 uppercase rules in me-hub.css, 8 in perks-shop.css, 7 in gigs-detail.css); numbered scaffolding none (the numbers are real steps and rungs); text overflow: none at 320, but at 200% text the board scrolls horizontally to 431px.

**Deterministic scan.** CLI over `src`: 6 findings, **5 false positives, 1 true positive**. False: `broken-image` at `ImageSlot.jsx:8` (regex matched `<img>` in a comment; the real tag only renders with a src, else a labelled slot); four `side-tab` hits (`admin.css:232`, `checkout-sheet.css:122`, `gigs-detail.css:359`, `me-hub.css:197`) are 8–9px rotated-square CSS chevrons, not card accents. True: `layout-transition` at `application.css:225` — the tracker's `.af__body` animates `max-height`. `src/pages`: clean. Browser (`detect.js` on 6 routes): 28 findings on 17 elements — `clipped-overflow-container` 7 (the board/shop cream panels and the drop pieces, which clip on purpose so folder tabs bite in), `layout-transition` 6 (the same single rule, reported per route), `overused-font` 5 (Instrument Sans at 28–43% of text — expected for a product body face, not a fault), `cramped-padding` 3 (the mission cover's h1 highlight span has 2px vertical padding for 21px text; the drop pieces' children sit flush), `tiny-text` 3 (Wallet ledger meta "Aug 22 · mission payout" at 10px), `skipped-heading` 3 (h1 → h3 on the mission page and shop; h2 → h4 in the drop), `all-caps-body` 1 (the 34-character uppercase mission title on the cover). Where the two agree: the detector's `all-caps-body`, `tiny-text` and `skipped-heading` are the machine's view of the eyebrow-and-display-h2 problem the review names. The detector caught two things the review missed: the 2px vertical padding on the highlighter title span, and the h1→h3 heading gap.

**Visual overlays.** Browser automation here is headless Playwright, so no user-visible overlay exists; the browser findings above are console output captured programmatically. Mutation preflight and injection succeeded on all six routes; the live server was started on port 8400 and stopped cleanly.

## Overall Impression

The object language works and the payout sheet is the best-designed moment in the app. What undercuts it is trust and lead: the board opens on brands and events instead of the dollar a student came for, two flows that touch money simulate success while the two that don't refuse to, and the small type (placeholders, Lacquer notes, 10px caps) is where the important numbers live. The single biggest opportunity is a board that answers "what can I finish before my next class?" with one tile before anything else.

## What's Working

1. **The withdraw sheet.** Amount, where, how fast, then the summary — payout order. Over-limit is a plain sentence with a disabled CTA; fees, ETA, legal name and W-9 appear only when they bite; the ledger gains a pending row. Every high-stakes flow should copy its shape.
2. **Paper objects that differ per object.** Folders with status tabs, notched tickets, a taped note holding the pay, highlighter blocks on covers, a flip clock. Four different things drawn four ways is what stops the system reading as a skin.
3. **Checkout structure and honesty.** Three questions, total pinned, back-a-step, card path hands off to the shop, credit named with its shop value in the split note, errors held until the student tries to place.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Money flows simulate success; the honest flows refuse to.** Withdraw mints "Ref AX-W-… · arrives by Mon, Sep 14" and debits the balance; checkout says "Order in — tracking hits your inbox by tonight". Apply and Save-a-seat say "Nothing was sent." *Why:* a student who cashes out $20 believes Venmo money is coming; the inconsistency also makes the honest sheets look like bugs. *Fix:* one policy — either every commit ends in the same disclosure ("Nothing moved — cash-outs open at launch; your $20 stays in the wallet") and no reference numbers, or Wallet/Cart/Checkout carry a persistent "Example wallet · nothing is charged" line. *Command:* `/impeccable harden`, then `/impeccable clarify`.

**[P1] The board does not lead with cash or answer "today".** First viewport: Brands (6 discs), Events (8 cards), then MISSIONS, tabs, chips, one tile whose $25 lands ~715px down. Tiles show `$25 +80 XP` but no duration or deadline (`hours`, `deadlineOn` exist in the data). *Note:* brands-first was the owner's explicit decision on 2026-09-08/09; this is the review's judgement against it, recorded for the owner to weigh. *Fix (if taken):* missions first, one "worth your time today" tile above the tabs ("$25 · 45 min · by Sun"), `duration · deadline` as a second caps line on every tile, brands as a row under the first tiles. *Command:* `/impeccable shape` (board lead), then `/impeccable layout`.

**[P1] White on the coral band fails AA against the project's own rule.** Invite's title and note are white on `#F2542D`: 3.45:1 (needs 4.5). `sub-screen.css` sets `--sh-ink: var(--gray-0)` for coral while DESIGN.md names white-on-coral as the don't. *Fix:* `.sub__band--coral { --sh-ink: var(--ink-900); --sh-note: var(--violet-800); }`, as blush/pink/orange already do. *Command:* `/impeccable polish`.

**[P2] Small type carries the important numbers.** Placeholders measure ~2.6:1 (DS-owned `--gray-400`); hints, errors and "NEEDED" are set in Lacquer; the R8 distances on Me ("5,620 XP to Syndicate") and Levels are Lacquer; Wallet ledger meta is 10px (detector `tiny-text`); the Withdraw control is 10px caps. *Fix:* in the design system (Principle 5) placeholder → a 4.5:1 muted ink, hint/error in the body face; in the app, set `hub__xp-away`, `lv__step-away` and ledger meta on the `--text-xs` rung, keep one Lacquer phrase per view. *Command:* `/impeccable typeset` (app side); a DS change for the input.

**[P2] The cart FAB parks over the shop's right-hand controls.** At 320×568 it covers the search glyph; at 390 its x-band is the right column's "+" band, so every right-column add button passes under it. Two bags (FAB and Perks tab) mean two destinations. *Fix:* retire the FAB — count badge on the Perks tab's bag and a second tap opens the cart, or a "Cart · 2" chip beside Filter; if it stays, move it off the card-control band and hide it while the tools row is in view. *Command:* `/impeccable layout`.

## Persona Red Flags

**Casey (one-handed, distracted):** Back is a bare top-left chevron on cart and every sub-screen. The FAB sits over the right column's add buttons. Two horizontal scrollers (brands, events) stack above the vertical list, so a wrong-axis flick at the top does nothing. Withdraw is a 10px underlined word. Tapping the tab-bar bag expecting the cart lands on the shop. The Apply pill is right-side and 44px — that one is right.

**Jordan (first-timer):** Unlabeled tab icons (flag = Gigs?). A flame with no label or count. Six coloured letters under BRANDS, one truncated to "Axelerate …". "Perks" for a shop, "Syndicate", "+1 freeze", "K-beauty" beside Digital and Physical. IN REVIEW vs SUBMITTED unexplained. "Credits 2,400" with no dollar value on Me and Wallet. Sign-up's only guidance is handwriting. Then Apply says the product is not launched.

**Sam (screen reader / keyboard / 4.5:1):** Focus ring visible, dialogs trap focus and Escape closes, toasts are `role=status`, tab bar and discs have full names — good. Then ~24 Tab stops before the first mission; Invite title 3.45:1; placeholders ~2.6:1; errors and R8 distances in Lacquer; Withdraw at 10px; the events carousel is not focusable as a scroller.

**Student between classes, 90 seconds:** Opens the board, sees brand initials and two events (one dated yesterday), scrolls, finds "$25, +80 XP" with no minutes and no deadline, taps, reads a deadline three days past, taps Apply, reads "Applications open at launch." Puts the phone down without an answer — and the board never used the word "today".

## Minor Observations

- No expiry state: Solra's deadline Sep 6 and the Founder AMA on Sep 8 display as live on Sep 9; nothing compares `deadlineOn` or event `date` to now. The "This mission has ended" page exists for slugs off the board; tiles need the same.
- "Delete account" (`Settings.jsx`) has no handler — a red destructive button that does nothing.
- Settings' Name / School / Email rows read as fields but are read-only; nothing says how to change them.
- Wallet says "2,400 pts"; everywhere else the unit is "credit".
- DynaPuff as h2 on board, shop, wallet against DESIGN.md's "one per screen, the h1"; detector: h1→h3 on the mission page and shop, h2→h4 in the drop.
- The cover title's highlight span has 2px vertical padding at 21px (detector `cramped-padding`); the tracker's `.af__body` animates `max-height` (detector; comment says intentional for the a11y tree — animate `grid-template-rows` or transform instead).
- At 200% text the board overflows horizontally to 431px; at 320 brand names truncate to "Axe…", "Not…".
- Brand page is two rows with no description or mark, reachable from every disc and host line.
- Tracker folders have no "what happens next" per status; Paid folders do not link to the wallet row they produced.
- Levels gate strings are two lines of 10px caps.
- PDP shows "883 left" and a stepper above the promises row, before any decision — inventory-admin tone.
- The "»" glyph ends most primary CTAs — a house tic that reads as an arrow substitute.
- Hover and `prefers-reduced-motion` were not exercised.

## Questions to Consider

1. If the first two blocks a student sees are Brands and Events, who is the board for — the student PRODUCT.md describes, or a brand-sales screenshot?
2. The app refuses to fake an application but mints a reference number for a fake $20 payout. Which is the policy, and why does the more dangerous one get the exception?
3. R1 has been overridden on the mission dock, Me, Wallet and Invite. Is "credit never stands alone" still a principle, or should PRODUCT.md list the four places it does not hold?
4. If DynaPuff is on every h2, what does the h1 have left to say "this is the screen you are on"?
5. A locked mission states its distance but cannot be opened. If the number turns a wall into a target, why can't the student look at the target?
6. What would the board look like if it answered exactly one question — "what can I finish before my next class?" — with one tile, and everything else came second?
